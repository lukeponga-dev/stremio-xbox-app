## Full description
OpenStream TV is an independent media discovery and playback application for Windows and compatible TV devices.

Discover movies and series through supported catalogues, keep track of titles in your personal library, and connect compatible third-party add-ons and streaming services to find available playback sources. The interface is designed for the big screen, with clear navigation and controller-friendly browsing.

OpenStream TV does not host or provide movie or television content. Catalogues, add-ons, stream availability, and playback services are supplied by third parties and may vary. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to access the content you choose to play.

OpenStream TV is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of Stremio, Microsoft, or Xbox.
